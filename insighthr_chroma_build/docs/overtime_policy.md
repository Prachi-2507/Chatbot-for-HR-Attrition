# Overtime Policy

**Effective Date:** January 2025
**Applies to:** All full-time non-exempt employees

## Overview
Employees who work beyond their standard 40-hour work week are eligible for overtime compensation in accordance with local labor regulations.

## Eligibility
- Non-exempt employees are eligible for overtime pay at 1.5x their base hourly rate for hours worked beyond 40 in a single week.
- Exempt employees (managers, directors, and specialists at Grade 5 and above) are not eligible for overtime pay but may be granted compensatory time off at manager discretion.

## Approval Process
1. Overtime must be pre-approved by a direct manager before it is worked, except in emergency operational situations.
2. Unapproved overtime may not be compensated and should be reported to HR immediately.
3. Sales department employees working overtime during quarter-end close do not require pre-approval, given the predictable seasonal nature of this work.

## Monitoring and Well-being
Employees who consistently log more than 10 hours of overtime per week for four consecutive weeks will be flagged for a mandatory workload review with HR and their manager. Research suggests sustained high overtime is associated with lower work-life balance scores and higher attrition risk, particularly in the Sales and Research & Development departments.
