# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

**Effective Date:** January 2025
**Applies to:** All full-time employees

## Accrual
- Employees accrue 1.75 PTO days per month worked, totaling 21 days annually.
- Employees with over 10 years of tenure accrue an additional 5 days annually (26 total).

## Carryover
Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried into the following calendar year. Remaining unused days beyond this cap are forfeited unless local law requires payout.

## Requesting Time Off
1. Requests should be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance through the HR portal.
2. Sales department requests during the last two weeks of a fiscal quarter require additional manager approval due to quarter-end workload.
3. Emergency leave (bereavement, medical) does not require advance notice; notify your manager and HR as soon as possible.

## Departmental Considerations
Research & Development lab-based roles should coordinate PTO with lab scheduling to ensure equipment and experiments have appropriate coverage.
