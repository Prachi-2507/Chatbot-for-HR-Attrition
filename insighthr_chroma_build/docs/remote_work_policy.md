# Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

**Effective Date:** March 2025
**Applies to:** All departments

## Work Arrangement Options
1. **Fully Remote** — Available to employees whose role does not require regular on-site presence, subject to manager approval.
2. **Hybrid** — Employees split time between office and remote work, typically 3 days in-office, 2 days remote.
3. **Fully On-Site** — Required for roles involving lab work, manufacturing, or physical equipment (e.g., Lab Technicians, Manufacturing Directors).

## Eligibility by Department
- **Sales**: Hybrid by default due to client travel requirements; fully remote may be granted for Sales Executives with a strong quarterly performance record.
- **Research & Development**: Lab-based roles (Lab Technician, Research Scientist) require on-site presence for equipment access. Research Directors and Managers may work hybrid.
- **Human Resources**: Hybrid or fully remote available for all roles, given the administrative nature of the work.

## Distance From Home
Employees whose commute distance exceeds 15 units are automatically eligible to request hybrid or remote arrangements, regardless of department, to reduce commute burden and support retention.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop, monitor, and a one-time $300 home office stipend for employees approved for hybrid or fully remote arrangements.

## Review
Remote work arrangements are reviewed annually alongside performance reviews and may be adjusted based on business needs.
