# Retention & Engagement Policy

**Effective Date:** June 2025
**Owner:** Human Resources

## Purpose
This policy outlines the company's approach to reducing voluntary attrition and improving employee engagement, particularly in departments and roles identified as higher-risk through HR analytics.

## Risk Factors Monitored by HR
Based on historical attrition analysis, the following factors are most strongly associated with voluntary departure:
- Low job satisfaction scores (rating of 1 or 2 out of 4)
- Poor work-life balance scores (rating of 1 or 2 out of 4)
- Frequent business travel combined with entry-level compensation
- Tenure under 2 years, particularly in Sales Representative and Lab Technician roles

## Intervention Program
1. **Quarterly Pulse Surveys** — All employees complete a short engagement survey; scores below threshold trigger a manager check-in.
2. **Stay Interviews** — Employees with under 2 years of tenure in high-risk roles are offered a voluntary "stay interview" with HR to surface concerns early.
3. **Compensation Review** — Sales Representatives and Lab Technicians, identified as higher-turnover roles, are reviewed annually against market benchmarks to ensure competitive pay.
4. **Manager Training** — Managers of departments with attrition rates above the company average receive mandatory retention-focused coaching.

## Escalation
Departments or job roles with attrition rates exceeding 20% in a rolling 12-month period trigger a mandatory root-cause review conducted jointly by HR and department leadership.
